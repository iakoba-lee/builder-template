**Date:** 2026-10-08
**Group:** D: agency client (earlier also B: a family member took over his inbox)
**How I found them:** My business partner. This is the creator behind two of the agency's clients.
**Persona:** [Sponsored-link influencer](../personas.md#sponsored-link-influencer)
**Recorded:** no. Reconstructed from my memory on 2026-10-12, so nothing below is verbatim.

> **Bias note:** He co-owns the agency, so his view of "after" is not neutral. Weight his
> "before" story (doing it himself, then his spouse doing it) more than his view of the agency.

## What I asked / what happened

He said he never planned to have anyone manage his inbox. He didn't see how much handling
sponsorships himself was hurting him until his spouse said she was going to take it over. She
had noticed he was missing deadlines, replying slowly, and damaging brand relationships.

Once she took over, he made more videos with less effort, hit his deadlines, and earned much
more. He said it was hard to see the problem until he was "out of the fire" (my recollection of
his framing; I recall no exact wording).

That worked until she got busy with other things. She started falling behind too, and the
business suffered again. Then he and I started working together.

## What they actually do today

The agency (me) manages the sponsorship inbox, negotiation, and deadlines. He films. In my first
full month handling it, I closed a strong month of deals. (The
figure is kept out of this public repo.)

## Pains

- Missed brand deadlines while running his own inbox. He named no cost, only that the business
  suffered.
- Slow replies to brands, which damaged brand relationships. No cost named.
- Inbox work took time away from making videos, so he made fewer of them. Earnings rose
  "a lot" once someone else took it over (no number given).
- He couldn't see the problem from inside it. Someone close to him had to name it.
- A helper fixed it only while she had spare time. When she got busy, the same pains came back.

## Solutions they've tried

| Tried | Still using? | Why it fell short |

| Spouse took over the inbox | No | Worked well until she got busy, then she fell behind too |

## Strongest signal

**He didn't recognize the problem himself; someone else had to point it out.** If that's
typical, creators who most need help won't search for "sponsorship management." Content should
name the symptoms they'd recognize: missed deadlines, slow replies, brands that stop
responding.

Second signal: **a helper with limited time is not a fix that lasts.** The spouse solved the
problem until her own time ran out. That supports the case for an agency over "have a friend or
family member handle it."
