## What I asked / what happened

Walked them through the client HQ in Notion (deal tracker + dashboard), compared their average
deal value to [Ben Kimball AI], proposed raising rates, explained 20% organic / 30% referral,
and chased awaiting-payment rows. They confirmed channel links, address for shipping, and that
WhatsApp is fine for ops.

## What they actually do today

- Tech / phone review creator. Roughly **250K+** followers across platforms. Posts about **once
  or twice a day**. Auto-crossposts Instagram → Facebook; Threads also exists.
- Already closing brand deals (phone cases / accessory brands named on the call). Average deal
  value about **$300** (vs about **$800** on [Ben Kimball AI], which [Interviewer] said is
  similar or slightly smaller).
- Some weeks show **~13 legit inquiries** on the dashboard rebuilt from their history, with only
  **1–2 closes**.
- [Interviewer] had found about **60** old email threads that were opened but never closed, and
  was re-pinging those brands.
- Still in school (entrepreneurship program); almost done — one semester after the current one.

## Pains

- **Underpricing without a system.** "$300… It's just random, honestly." Cost: hundreds of
  dollars per deal vs a similar-size AI/tech peer channel at ~$800.
- **Low close rate on real inbound.** Double-digit inquiry weeks, single-digit closes.
- **Sponsorship vs organic balance.** "I just don't wanna like spam ads since I only post like
  once or twice a day."
- **Incomplete payment memory.** Several deals in "awaiting payment"; they need to check PayPal
  / history before [Interviewer] can chase brands.
- **Ops friction on mobile Notion.** No easy search while checking PayPal; prefers desktop for
  real use.

## Strongest signal

**Even a tech creator with real inbound underprices and under-closes.** The dashboard made the
gap visible (13 opportunities → 1–2 closes; $300 vs ~$800 peer). The limit they name is
**content volume / fear of looking too sponsored**, not "I don't know what an email is." That
is Breakout-ish admin and pricing, not Emerging "don't know the steps."
