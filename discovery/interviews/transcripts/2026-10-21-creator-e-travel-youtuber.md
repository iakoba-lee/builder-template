## Summary

[Creator E] has made travel and challenge content for about eight years, mainly on YouTube, while
working three other creative jobs. Sponsorship income is rare; most months are AdSense only.
Their inbox gets volume, but "legit" brand emails are uncommon and much of it is scam, TikTok
Shop, or affiliate. They time-block email every few days, flag what looks real, and leave most
of it. They want to quit the other jobs once YouTube makes $5,000 a month for six months
straight. This call was largely a sales and onboarding conversation; they agreed to try the
agency (20% inbound / 30% referral or outbound).
