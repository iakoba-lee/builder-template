## What I asked / what happened

He films his grandmother's gardening and floral videos and treats the account as a test client
for his newsletter agency. The audience is growing fast on Instagram, with some YouTube Shorts
and TikTok. He wanted to learn how social sponsorships work.

## What they actually do today

- Instagram is the main platform. He reposts only the best videos to YouTube Shorts (about 800
  subscribers) and TikTok (about 3,000 followers).
- Posting depends on his spare time. It's "technically outside of the scope of like my main
  package."
- Makes money from a $30 digital product and a TikTok Shop affiliate code from a local
  wildflower brand.
- Had "a handful" of sponsor emails he hadn't opened. No email address in the bio.

## Pains

- **Doesn't know how social sponsorships work.** "I just don't know the game on how it works."
- **Afraid of turning the account into a sales channel.** "My grandma is not like, hey, go buy
  our stuff. And so I'm hesitant to like play the affiliate game."
- **No sense of price.** "How steep can I go… a couple hundred bucks. Or is it like a couple
  thousand bucks?"
- **Leaves inbound emails unopened.** "I haven't like dove into them."

## Solutions they've tried

| Tried | Still using? | Why it fell short |
|---|---|---|
| Affiliate code (TikTok Shop) | Yes | Feels salesy for the brand; small |
| $30 digital product | Launching | Making new products takes a lot of work |
| Newsletter sponsorship model he knows | Not applied yet | Doesn't know how it works on social |

## Their words

- "I just don't know the game on how it works"
- "I'm hesitant to like play the affiliate game"
- "how steep can I go when it comes to these packages?"
- "we've got a handful of those emails. I haven't like dove into them"

## Strongest signal

**He's an experienced marketer and still stuck.** He runs an agency, knows how newsletter
sponsorships work, and has an audience. Not knowing the process for social is the barrier, not
general business skill.
