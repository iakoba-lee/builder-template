## What they actually do today

  don't have a clear TikTok approach outside clipping.

- **Long-form:** only a couple posts in the last year; working on one to publish in the next few
  days and aiming for **monthly**.
- **Clipping:** learned from an **impersonation account** that stole their YouTube and went viral

  (intro in front of every part + captions). One trial reel ~**22M views** after about a week,
  still cooking.
- **Inbox:** separate email used **mostly for sponsorships** (not personal mail) — easier for
  agency access.
