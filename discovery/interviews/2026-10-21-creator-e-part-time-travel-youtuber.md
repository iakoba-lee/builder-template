## What I asked / what happened

Background, posting cadence, income, how brand deals arrive, what the inbox looks like, and
whether they want inbox management. They agreed to try the agency; next step was agreement,
email access, dashboard, and a follow-up call on rates and strategy.

## What they actually do today

- ~8 years creating; YouTube is the main bet. Travel / personality-driven challenge content
  (example: car camping in a "spookiest town" shoot). Aiming for about one long-form a month.
- Part-time creator. Three other creative jobs: social for a coffee shop, social for a light
  shop (~20 hrs/week combined on those two), and editing a sports podcast.
- Income: usually YouTube Partner Program only, about **$200–500/month**. Occasional brand-deal
  months around **$1,500**. Best month they named: **~$3,500**. Short-form money is rare;
  TikTok "used to be really good… now it's flat out bad."
- Brand deals: almost all inbound. "Very little to no success" cold-reaching brands. Recent
  inbound traction with eSIM companies and email talks with [a major online travel brand] after
  a post they liked (pitching ideas for the rest of the year / next six months).
- Inbox: "a good amount" of email, but **"a legit one, not that often."** Clouded with TikTok
  Shop, scams (they screenshot and ask someone if it's real), and affiliate / commission offers.
  They time-block every few days, flag a few, leave the rest: "most of the time I'm not touching
  my inbox."

## Pains

- **Not enough creator income to quit the other jobs.** "It's simply because I don't make enough
  money off it." Cost: three side jobs plus creator work; described a day with a video deadline
  and six client posts due.
- **Inbox is mostly fluff and risk.** Scam / TikTok Shop / affiliate volume; few flat-rate
  sponsorships. "why are we allergic to flat rates?"
- **Cold outreach doesn't work for them.** Inbound is the only path that lands.
- **Agency skepticism.** "I've heard stories and I'm very skeptical about working with
  agencies, but I do need it."
- **Fake brand emails** (example: fake DJI). "please, can one of these be freaking real?"

## Strongest signal

**Outside tech/AI, the "don't know the steps" and "inbox swamp" patterns both show up, but the
money problem is "not enough real deals," not "too many to process."** They already time-block
email and know scams from affiliates; what they lack is consistent flat-rate inbound and someone
to extract the few real ones. The quit-number is concrete: **$5k/month for six months**.

Also: they are **skeptical of agencies and still said yes** because "I do need it" — same trust
bar the sprint-2 brand note flagged.
