## What I asked / what happened

Before working with me he had never had a sponsorship and didn't know how the process worked.
Like the new client we onboarded on 2026-10-08, he didn't do anything about sponsorships because
he didn't know what he needed.

Before he started making content, he tried having an AI agent manage his work email. It never
worked well, he gave up on it soon after, and he wasn't looking forward to figuring it out again.

He wants to hear how I got my agents working in the inbox.

He had no work email until he signed with me. Creating one and adding it to the bio on each of his
social accounts were part of onboarding. I now handle the inbox, pricing, and negotiation. He
has a few small first deals so far.

## Strongest signal

**Not knowing the process stopped him from starting at all.** Before the agency he had no work
email, nothing in his bio, no rates, and no package list, so brands had no way to reach him.
The new client from 2026-10-08 was in the same place. For creators who have never had a deal,
the first barrier is not knowing the steps, not the work of running an inbox.

Second signal: **he wants to understand the AI tooling, not just hand it off.** The first time he
set it up himself, it failed. That could be a teaching or content angle ("how our agents work in
the inbox").
