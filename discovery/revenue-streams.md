# Agency revenue streams

**Sources:** Aspen coaching sessions (2026-10-22); weekly recap finished 2026-10-25.  
**Status:** streams described; **sequencing and per-stream targets not yet written down.**

Ties to [`problem-bet.md`](problem-bet.md) (Breakout management as paid core; Emerging via
content/Skool) and [`customer-avatars.md`](customer-avatars.md) (tiers by brand-deal revenue).

## 1. Core service — one-on-one sponsor management

- **Model:** commission only. **20%** organic / inbound deals; **30%** agency-brought (referrals
  / outbound). No lock-in.
- **Goal (stated):** 10–20 creators and **$25–50K MRR**, enough for Jacob to work on the agency
  full-time.
- **Who it fits:** Breakout (and Established) creators with real inbound — the Sprint 3 primary
  bet.

## 2. Skool (CreatorOS)

- **~900 members** and a warm lead list. **[Partner] owns it.**
- **Ideas:** paid Skool tier; SaaS / retainer upsells.
- **Role:** serve creators **too small to sign for management** (Emerging / pre-inbound).
- **Milestone:** first Skool live with [Partner] was **2026-10-24**.

## 3. Brand outreach

- Move negotiations from email to **short video calls** with brand marketing contacts; calls can
  also open **multi-creator** deals.
- **LinkedIn content** positioning Jacob to brands as a manager who connects them with
  influencers.
- A **roster** of creators with media kits ready to pitch.
- **Brand-paid multi-creator campaigns** (e.g. 20–50 videos): agency coordinates and takes a
  large cut, alongside one-on-one management. A brand at IFA said they prefer buying this way.
- **Cross-refer** brands between clients in the same niche at **30%**.
