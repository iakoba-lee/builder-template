# Custom Adventurist sponsorship inbox, Jun 1 – Sep 26, 2026

Aggregates from an audit of every sponsorship email thread in Custom Adventurist's business inbox.
Copied from the agency's Obsidian vault (`Custom Adventurist/Sandbox/CA-inbox-data/`).

The row-level files (`ca_sponsorship_master_…csv`, `audit/missed_135_prescreen.csv`) stay in the
vault and are not committed here. They contain brand contact names, emails, and Gmail thread
links.

## Files

| File | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| `opportunity_statuses.csv` | Opportunities first seen since June 1, by current status |
| `opportunity_types.csv` | The same opportunities, by inquiry type |
| `weekly_opportunities.csv` | New opportunities per week, with wins and open follow-ups. The week of Sep 21 is partial. |

## What the numbers say

Figures below come from the row-level master file (1,349 opportunities, including 279 open
before June 1) unless marked otherwise.

- **Volume is steady and growing.** New opportunities went from about 40 a week in June to 60–100
  a week by late August.
- **Nearly all of it is inbound.** 1,329 of 1,349 threads started with the brand. The creator
  isn't short of deals; the problem is handling them.
- **Deals are being dropped.** 135 opportunities never got a reply. 70 of those were more than 60
  days old at the cutoff, and 101 were paid-sponsorship requests.
- **Follow-ups are piling up.** Open follow-ups per week climbed from under 5 in June to 46–48 in
  late September (`weekly_opportunities.csv`).
- **Most outreach comes through middlemen.** 791 threads came via an agency or network rather than
  the brand directly.
- **Why deals were lost:** bad fit (216), budget (52), couldn't agree on terms (20), creator
  declined (15).
- **Deal size:** 99 deals have a recorded agreed value. The median is $1,300, the range is
  $300–$4,300, and the total is about $152K.

These back up the inbox, follow-up, and pricing pains in [customer-avatars.md](../../customer-avatars.md)
for the Established tier.
