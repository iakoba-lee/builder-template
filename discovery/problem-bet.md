# Sprint 3 one-pager: primary bet, evidence, smallest test

**Written:** 2026-10-26  
**Plan:** `sprints/sprint-3-plan.md`  
**Revenue context:** [`revenue-streams.md`](revenue-streams.md) (Aspen 10/22 + weekly recap 10/25)

## Primary bet

**Breakout-shaped creators who already get brand mail: inbox/admin + pricing.**

The agency’s paid work is taking over inbound, raising rates, and closing faster — not teaching
first-time creators the basics. “Don’t know the steps” still shows up (Emerging, beginners), but
it is the **top of funnel / content** path, not the core offer we get paid for.

## Buyer

Tiers are by **monthly brand-deal revenue**, not followers. Full avatars:
[`customer-avatars.md`](customer-avatars.md).

- **Primary — Breakout ($5–15K/month brand deals):** core buyer. Real inbound, messy inbox,
  gut-feel rates, underpriced short-form/usage. Ready when they lack a rate card, fumble a deal,
  learn a peer charges more, or pass ~2–3 deals/month. Best pitch: brand referral pool.
- **Emerging ($0–5K):** top of funnel. Day job/school common; don’t know pitch/rates; no media
  kit. Stay in touch until inbound shows up, then audit. Not the paid core until they grow toward
  Breakout.
- **Established ($15K+):** volume and leverage (Custom Adventurist). Pitch rates/terms via
  referral; not the Sprint 3 content test.

## Smallest test

**Publish a short pricing content series** aimed at creators who already get inbound (not a
beginner checklist as the first test).

- 3 posts: how undercharging shows up, what a flat-rate floor looks like by niche, what “13
  inquiries / 1 close” means.
- Success = outbound DMs or calls from creators who already have brand mail — not vanity views.
- Run in parallel with what we already do for clients (rate card + inbox for R and E). That ops
  work is delivery, not the test.
- Deal prep for those closes: use the seven-element frame in
  [`negotiation-framework.md`](negotiation-framework.md) (criteria + options basket, not gut
  numbers).

**Not the test:** build a media kit, or more link-page features.

## Call on the link page

**Pause further building as a product focus.**

- **Keep** the multi-tenant page as an **agency deliverable** when a client needs an on-domain
  link list (Custom Adventurist–style).
- **Do not** spend the next sprint on link editing, analytics, or media-kit product work unless
  a paying client or brand asks for a named metric the page does not provide.
- **Revisit** if a brand says they need click proof from the creator’s page, or a creator pays
  (or renews) specifically for those numbers.

## What would change this bet

- Two more outside-tech/AI creators who clearly need setup coaching and have **no** inbound.
- A brand that says they will not work with talent managers / “the creator’s team,” or that
  requires link-page click data to renew.
