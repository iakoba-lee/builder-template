# Sprint 3 Plan

**Goal:** Know which of the two creator problems (doesn't know the steps vs. inbox/admin swamp) is
the agency's primary bet, with evidence from outside the current tech/AI client circle plus at
least one stronger brand conversation, and write that up as a one-page problem / evidence /
smallest test.

**Why this:** Sprint 2 didn't get the answers I was looking for: who the ideal target is, and why.
This sprint is about getting that answer.

**Done looks like:**

- The three already-scheduled interviews are done and recorded (Sprint 2's weak spot was notes
  from memory only).
- 2+ creators outside tech/AI and 1–2 brands interviewed. Brands are asked: do they want click
  data from a creator's link page? Do they lump talent managers in with the agencies they're tired
  of?
- `discovery/synthesis.md` (or the insights doc) is updated with what changed.
- A committed one-pager naming the buyer (e.g. Breakout and inbox-swamped vs. Emerging and "don't
  know the steps"), the evidence, and the smallest test: a pricing content series, a beginner
  checklist offer, or a non-software process test, not "build media kit."
- An explicit call on the link page: keep it as an agency deliverable, pause further building, or
  drop it as a focus, with a recommendation that makes a real choice.

**Known side work (not the sprint goal):** Link editing and archiving for Custom Adventurist's
brand deals: edit a link without losing its click history, and archive past links with their
traction. Client-driven; tracked here so it isn't counted as drift.

**Predicted difficulty:** 2
