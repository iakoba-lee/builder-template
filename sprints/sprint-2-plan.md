# Sprint 2 Plan

**Goal:** Have data that points to what pains creators experience getting sponsorships and the
solutions they have already tried.

**Why this:** It tells my partner and me what kind of content to make to attract customers and
what language creators use to describe their problems. It also follows through on the Sprint 1
retro: understand the process and the people who feel the pain before the next big build. The
internal process audit is done; interviews expand it.

**Done looks like:**

- 5–10 interviews completed, recorded and transcribed, with a mix of:
  - creators who handle sponsorships themselves,
  - at least one creator who has hired someone to do it,
  - creators who have hired an agency,
  - the agency's current clients — what they struggled with, and what is different now that
    the agency is helping them,
  - any brands I can reach, about their side of working with creators and agencies on campaigns.
- Anonymized notes for each interview committed to `discovery/` (raw transcripts with real names
  stay out of the repo).
- A written synthesis of all the interviews committed to `discovery/`: the pains, the solutions
  people have tried, and the language they use to describe their problems.

**Predicted difficulty:** 3
